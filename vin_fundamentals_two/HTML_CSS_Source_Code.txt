<h1 class="page-title">Our programmes</h1><p>Mamba Community's proposed programme areas respond to common community development priorities. The descriptions below are illustrative and would need validation by the organisation before publication.</p>
<section><h2>Education and skills</h2><img class="feature-img" src="assets/learning-community.svg" alt="Illustration showing a facilitator and learners taking part in a group learning session.">
<p>Activities may include reading support, digital literacy sessions, mentorship and practical skills workshops for young people and adults.</p>
<h3>Example activities</h3><ol><li>Identify learning needs with residents and local schools.</li><li>Organise accessible workshops and mentoring sessions.</li><li>Collect attendance and participant feedback with consent.</li></ol></section>
<section><h2>Health awareness</h2><p>Possible activities include community health education, referral information and awareness sessions delivered with qualified local partners. The organisation should not publish personal health information.</p></section>
<section><h2>Environment and livelihoods</h2><img class="feature-img" src="assets/tree-planting.svg" alt="Illustration of two volunteers planting a young tree in a green community space.">
<p>Activities may include tree planting, clean-up days, waste reduction education and community-led environmental planning.</p></section>
<section><h2>Key terms</h2><dl><dt>Community-based organisation (CBO)</dt><dd>A locally rooted organisation formed to address needs or priorities in a community.</dd><dt>Monitoring</dt><dd>Routine collection and review of information about activities and progress.</dd><dt>Outcome</dt><dd>A change or benefit experienced by people or communities as a result of an intervention.</dd></dl></section>

<p class="small-note">Progress is calculated as illustrative achievement divided by illustrative target. Before real publication, the organisation should state the reporting period, data source, collection method and any limitations.</p>
<h2>How to interpret the data</h2><ul><li>Targets show the planned level of activity.</li><li>Achievements show the example number recorded.</li><li>Progress makes comparison easier, but does not by itself prove long-term impact.</li></ul>
<div class="callout"><strong>Data quality note:</strong> Real programme reports should be checked by the responsible programme officer, dated, and supported by verifiable records. Personal or identifiable participant data should not be published without a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards.</div>
