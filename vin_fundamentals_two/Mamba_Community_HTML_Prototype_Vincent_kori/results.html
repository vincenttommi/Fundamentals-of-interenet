Progress is calculated as illustrative achievement divided by illustrative target. Before real publication, the organisation should state the reporting period, data source, collection method and any limitations.

How to interpret the data

  • Targets show the planned level of activity.
  • Achievements show the example number recorded.
  • Progress makes comparison easier, but does not by itself prove long-term impact.
Data quality note: Real programme reports should be checked by the responsible programme officer, dated, and supported by verifiable records. Personal or identifiable participant data should not be published without a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards.
