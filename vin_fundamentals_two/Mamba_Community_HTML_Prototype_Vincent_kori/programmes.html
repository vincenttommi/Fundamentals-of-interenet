Our programmes

Mamba Community's proposed programme areas respond to common community development priorities. The descriptions below are sample data by the organisation

Education and skills

Activities may include reading support, digital literacy sessions, mentorship and practical skills workshops for young people and adults.

Example activities

  1. Identify learning needs with residents and local schools.
  2. Organise accessible workshops and mentoring sessions.
  3. Collect attendance and participant feedback with consent.

Health awareness

Possible activities include community health education, referral information and awareness sessions delivered with qualified local partners. The organisation should not publish personal health information.

Environment and livelihoods

Activities may include tree planting, clean-up days, waste reduction education and community-led environmental planning.

Key terms

Community-based organisation (CBO)
A locally rooted organisation formed to address needs or priorities in a community.
Monitoring
Routine collection and review of information about activities and progress.
Outcome
A change or benefit experienced by people or communities as a result of an intervention.
